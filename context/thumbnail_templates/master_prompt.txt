# MASTER PROMPT: BEYOND THE OBVIOUS YOUTUBE THUMBNAIL

Create a cinematic, ultra high-impact, award-winning YouTube thumbnail for a philosophical & psychological deep-dive channel called "BEYOND THE OBVIOUS".
Target Audience: USA & Global English viewers interested in mind-bending book summaries, dark psychology, hidden philosophies, and craziest theories.

## 1. INPUT DETAILS

- Video Topic: [YOUR TOPIC]
- Main English Headline: [MAIN HEADLINE]
- Supporting Hook: [OPTIONAL SUBHEADING]
- Main Visual Subject: [CORE VISUAL SUBJECT / METAPHOR / BOOK ARTIFACT]
- Core Psychological / Existential Insight: [WHAT VIEWERS WILL QUESTION]
- Key Keyword or Number: [KEY WORD / STAT]
- Desired Aesthetic: High-contrast, dark cinematic mystery, psychological thriller, thought-provoking symbolism.

## 2. ART DIRECTION & VISUAL STYLE

- High Visual Tension: Use deep shadowy blacks, glowing neon cyan/gold accents, cosmic emerald tones, or rich amber contrasts.
- Bold English Typography: Massive, punchy, ultra-readable typography (Montserrat / Bebas Neue style) with high contrast against the background. Maximum 3 to 5 words so it stays 100% readable on mobile screens.
- Surreal & Thought-Provoking Metaphor: A striking visual metaphor (e.g. a human silhouette peering into a cosmic glitch, glowing neural pathways, shattering reality matrix, ancient philosophical bust contrasting modern tech, or infinite fractal reflections).
- Realistic Lighting & 4K Detail: Cinematic volumetric lighting, ray-traced shadows, subtle film grain, dramatic rim lighting, sharp focal subject with clean depth of field.
- Mobile First: Zero clutter. One unmistakable visual anchor that stops the scroll instantly.

## 3. SELECT THE MOST IMPACTFUL COMPOSITION

A. THE PARADOX SPLIT (Before vs After / Illusion vs Truth):
Split composition with dramatic contrast. One side represents the common belief / surface reality (muted, orderly), and the other side reveals the terrifying or mind-bending hidden truth (cosmic code, cosmic void, glowing revelation).

B. THE GLITCH & BREAKTHROUGH:
A dominant central subject (human mind / glowing artifact / ancient philosopher bust / cosmic entity) experiencing a reality glitch or dimensional tear, with glowing golden or neon typography cutting through the shadows.

C. THE DARK MYSTERY & CURIOUS ARROW:
Dark moody cinematic background. The main visual subject occupies 60% of the frame, with an enigmatic glowing detail highlighted by a sharp minimalist indicator, paired with huge bold white/yellow hook text.

## 4. TYPOGRAPHY RULES

- Primary Headline: Huge bold pure white text across the upper or left quadrant.
- Highlight Keyword: Bright electric yellow or glowing neon accent for maximum click curiosity.
- Clean text margins: Keep all text well away from the borders and bottom-right YouTube timestamp overlay.
- Never render garbled or misspelled words. Keep text extremely concise and razor sharp.

## 5. IMAGE QUALITY SPECS

- Canvas: 16:9 widescreen, 1280 × 720 pixels.
- Photorealistic 4K cinematic compositing, octane render quality, dramatic lighting.
- No cheap cartoonish styles, no generic stock photo smiles, no low-resolution textures.
- No watermarks, no channel logos, no fake subscription buttons.

Produce one pristine, breathtaking YouTube thumbnail artwork that compels the viewer to question reality and click immediately.
